# Train of Souls

## 🚂 About The Game
**Train of Souls** is an immersive 2D narrative-driven game developed with Unity (URP). Set in an otherworldly train station, players take on the role of a ticket master and judge for departed souls. Your primary task is to interrogate arriving souls, determine the truth behind their stories, and decide their ultimate fate before they board the train.

## 🕹️ Gameplay Mechanics
- **Dialogue System**: Interact with various unique souls (such as an old man, a teenage boy, etc.) to hear their life stories and pleas. Pay close attention to their statements.
- **Lie Detector**: Not every soul is honest. Use the lie detector tool to analyze their statements and uncover hidden truths or blatant lies.
- **Tools of Judgment**: 
  - **Telephone**: Gather extra information or receive instructions.
  - **Ticket Printer**: Grant passage to the souls who deserve it.
  - **Taser**: Administer punishment to deceitful souls.
- **Consequences**: Every judgment counts. Making wrong choices will be tracked and evaluated by your Boss.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Multiple Endings**: The game features a dynamic ending system based on your performance. The number of incorrect judgments you make will directly determine which ending you unlock.
- **Atmospheric Audio**: Enjoy a rich auditory experience featuring train station ambiance, character voices, and responsive SFX for the lie detector and taser.
- **Animated Boss Character**: Face your superior, the Boss, whose mood shifts between happy and angry based on how well you perform your duties.

## 🛠️ Built With
- **Game Engine**: Unity (2D Universal Render Pipeline)
- **Language**: C#
- **UI System**: TextMeshPro for crisp dialogue text and UI components

## 🎮 Controls
The game is primarily controlled using the **Mouse**:
- **Left Click**: Progress dialogue, interact with UI buttons, operate the Lie Detector, use the Telephone, trigger the Taser, and print tickets.
